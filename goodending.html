SUBJECT: ELIAS VILLAREAL

AGE: 13

SEX: MALE

LAST KNOWN LOCATION: ANGELICUM SCHOOL — SECOND FLOOR HALLWAY

FINAL STATUS: DECEASED — REMAINS RECOVERED

Elias Villareal was reported missing following an evening school event at Angelicum School on 14 OCT 2007. The initial investigation treated the disappearance as a possible abduction or unauthorized departure from school grounds.

Subsequent examination of the school revealed evidence of an unauthorized occult ritual conducted within an unused residential structure adjacent to the school grounds. Investigators now believe Villareal was brought to the location by an adult known to the school.

The markings documented during the original search were determined to form a ritual diagram. The handwritten note recovered from the subject's classroom contained a sequence of Latin phrases associated with the ritual and was being used as an instruction rather than a personal message.

Villareal's remains were subsequently located within the ritual site. A religious cross recovered from the apartment was found positioned over the remains during the final examination. Investigators believe this interrupted the ritual and prevented further disturbance of the site.

EVIDENCE 4 ITEMS — REVIEWED
EVIDENCE #001 — MOBILE PHONE

Villareal's mobile phone was recovered at approximately 06:17 on 15 OCT 2007 from the second floor hallway near the subject's classroom.

Recovered message fragments indicate that Villareal had been instructed to remain inside the school after the evening event. Additional messages reference a meeting and a location described as a "room below the old apartments."

EVIDENCE #002 — PHOTOGRAPH

The photograph was taken inside the residential structure later identified as the ritual site. Its original location was unknown during the first investigation because the structure had been omitted from the initial search records.

The photograph confirms the presence of a shrine and several objects matching the markings documented at Angelicum School.

EVIDENCE #003 — NOTE

The handwritten note is a partial ritual instruction written in Latin. The recovered text reads:

"Umbrae te vocant.
Nox te suscipit.
Audi vocem meam.
Surge ex tenebris.
Aperi portas.
Veni ad nos."

The phrases correspond to the sequence of actions documented at the ritual site. Investigators conclude that the note was intended to summon and contain an unidentified presence.

EVIDENCE #004 — AUDIO FILE

The 18-second recording recovered from Villareal's phone was digitally cleaned and reviewed.

The recording contains Villareal speaking the final lines of the note followed by a second unidentified voice. Investigators could not establish the identity of the second speaker.

The final audible phrase was identified as a repeated instruction to close the doors.

INTERNAL REPORTS FINAL ACCESS
INITIAL INCIDENT REPORT

Villareal was reported missing after failing to return home following an evening school event. The initial search failed to identify the apartment structure later confirmed as the ritual site.

The subject did not leave the school voluntarily. Evidence recovered from the classroom, phone, and apartment establishes that Villareal was lured from the classroom by an adult known to him.

FOLLOW-UP INVESTIGATION

The ritual site contained a shrine, a pentagram-like diagram, handwritten instructions, and personal effects belonging to Villareal.

The apartment had previously been used by Brother Mateo Reyes, a former religious instructor and maintenance supervisor at Angelicum School. Reyes had access to the school after hours and was familiar with the unused areas of the building and surrounding properties.

Investigators determined that Reyes had been conducting occult rituals intended to contact an entity he described in recovered writings as "the voice beyond the door."

RESTRICTED ADDENDUM — DECLASSIFIED

CAUSE OF DEATH: Villareal died during the ritual conducted by Brother Mateo Reyes.

RESPONSIBLE PARTY: BROTHER MATEO REYES

MOTIVE: Reyes believed Villareal was required as a final participant in a ritual intended to open a supernatural passage. Evidence indicates that Villareal was not a willing participant.

CASE CONCLUSION: Reyes was arrested after investigators connected him to the ritual site and recovered written records describing the events preceding Villareal's disappearance.

The ritual site was secured and all related materials were removed from the property. The case is considered solved.

Final investigator's note: No further disturbances have been reported since the recovered cross was placed over the remains.
