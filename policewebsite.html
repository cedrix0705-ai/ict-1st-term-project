SUBJECT: ELIAS VILLAREAL

AGE: 13

SEX: MALE

LAST KNOWN LOCATION: ANGELICUM SCHOOL — SECOND FLOOR HALLWAY

DATE LAST SEEN: 14 OCT 2007

Elias Villareal, age 13, was reported missing following an evening school event at Angelicum School on 14 OCT 2007. According to classmates and school personnel, Villareal was last seen leaving his classroom and entering the second floor hallway shortly before the school was closed for the evening.

An initial search of the school was conducted by faculty, security personnel, and responding officers. Classrooms, restrooms, stairwells, storage areas, and exterior grounds were searched. The subject was not located.

No confirmed evidence indicates that Villareal left the school through any known exit. Several areas of the second floor could not be adequately reviewed due to incomplete security footage and poor lighting conditions.

LAST KNOWN LOCATION: SECOND FLOOR HALLWAY — ANGELICUM SCHOOL
