SUBJECT: ELIAS VILLAREAL

DATE LAST SEEN: 14 OCT 2007

Elias Villareal, age 13, was reported missing following an evening school event at Angelicum School on 14 OCT 2007. According to classmates and school personnel, Villareal was last seen leaving his classroom and entering the second floor hallway shortly before the school was closed for the evening.

An initial search of the school was conducted by faculty, security personnel, and responding officers. Classrooms, restrooms, stairwells, storage areas, and exterior grounds were searched. The subject was not located.

Investigators also received an unverified report concerning an unused structure near the school grounds. The identity of the individual with access to this location remains ████████████████████.

INITIAL INCIDENT REPORT

Villareal was reported missing after failing to return home following an evening school event. School personnel reported that the subject had remained inside the building shortly before the disappearance.

Officers noted unusual markings in several areas of the building. No official connection to the disappearance was established at the time.

FOLLOW-UP INVESTIGATION

Investigators found no evidence indicating that Villareal exited the school through any confirmed entrance or exit. A search of the surrounding area produced no additional evidence.

A possible connection between the missing person case and an apparent occult group operating in the area was raised by investigators but could not be confirmed.
